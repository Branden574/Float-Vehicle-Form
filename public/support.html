How can we help?

Support for the Fleet Checkout web app and native iPhone and iPad app.

Contact Fleet Checkout Support

fleetcheckout.support@gmail.com

Include your app version, device model and a description of what happened. Never send your password or sign-in code. Remove personal or payment details from screenshots and receipts before emailing them.

Signing in

Use your staff email address and the newest six-digit code. Check your junk folder if the email is missing. After 60 seconds, use Resend code. Codes expire after 10 minutes and can be used once. Your fleet administrator manages driver authorization.

Set up a passkey on iPhone or iPad

  1. If Apple says you have no saved passkeys, close that sheet. In Fleet Checkout, enter your work email and sign in with the newest code.
  2. Within 10 minutes of signing in, open My profile → Create a passkey.
  3. Choose Passwords if asked where to save it. Confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. Look for Passkey saved in Fleet Checkout.
  4. Next time, choose Sign in with a passkey. On a shared iPad, use your own phone or the email code.

To use the same passkey on your personal iPhone and iPad, sign in to both with your Apple Account and turn on iCloud Passwords & Keychain sync. Apple’s iCloud Keychain setup guide.

Scanning gas receipts

In the native iPhone or iPad app, begin the vehicle return and open the Photographs step. Tap Scan gas receipt, allow camera access, scan each page, adjust the corners and save. Check that every page appears in the receipt list before submitting. Keep the app open while uploading; if an upload fails, reconnect and retry.

A vehicle is pending review

A pending return must be reviewed by an assigned reviewer. Contact your fleet administrator or reviewer to inspect the return and approve the vehicle’s availability. A driver cannot approve their own return.

Web and iPad records

Submitted records use the same fleet account on web and native apps. Refresh the other device to see the latest status. Resume an unfinished inspection on the same device with the same account within 24 hours of your last change. Drafts stay on that device.

Account and privacy questions

Open My profile → Delete my account in the native app or website to request permanent account and associated-data deletion. Keep your private receipt to check completion at Deletion status. Read the privacy policy for retention and data-use details. Contact support if you need help; never send sign-in codes.
