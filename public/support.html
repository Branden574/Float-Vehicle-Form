How can we help?

Support for the Fleet Checkout web app and native iPhone and iPad app.

Contact Fleet Checkout Support

fleetcheckout.support@gmail.com

Include your app version, device model and a description of what happened. Never send your password or sign-in code. Remove personal or payment details from screenshots and receipts before emailing them.

Signing in

Use your staff email address and the newest six-digit code. Check your junk folder if the email is missing. After 60 seconds, use Resend code. Codes expire after 10 minutes and can be used once. Your fleet administrator manages driver authorization.

Scanning gas receipts

In the native iPhone or iPad app, begin the vehicle return and open the Photographs step. Tap Scan gas receipt, allow camera access, scan each page, adjust the corners and save. Check that every page appears in the receipt list before submitting. Keep the app open while uploading; if an upload fails, reconnect and retry.

A vehicle is pending review

A pending return must be reviewed by an assigned reviewer. Contact your fleet administrator or reviewer to inspect the return and approve the vehicle’s availability. A driver cannot approve their own return.

Web and iPad records

Submitted records use the same fleet account on web and native apps. Refresh the other device to see the latest status. Unsubmitted drafts are held in the current app session and do not synchronize between devices.

Account and privacy questions

Contact the support email above for questions about your account or personal information. Do not include vehicle evidence or signed forms unless support asks for the specific information needed to resolve your request.
