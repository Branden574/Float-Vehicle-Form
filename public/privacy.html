Privacy · Updated November 9, 2026

Your information, explained.

Fleet Checkout is operated by Branden Vincent-Walker for authorized staff. This policy covers our web app and native iPhone and iPad app.

What we collect and why

We collect your name, email, charter/school, department, manager, account permissions and sign-in information to identify you, authorize fleet access and prefill forms. Vehicle inspections include mileage, dates, destinations, purpose, condition, damage marks, signatures and approval or correction history. Photos, scanned gas receipts and generated PDFs document your trip.

Receipts can contain purchase amounts, merchant details and partial payment information. Original photos may contain location and device metadata supplied by your camera. We do not continuously track your location. Avoid including unnecessary personal or payment information in uploads.

We also process request/security records, approximate connection information and error diagnostics to protect accounts, deliver the service and investigate failures. We do not sell personal information, use advertising trackers or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Who can access it

You can access your records. Authorized fleet administrators and assigned reviewers can access records needed to manage and approve trips. Configured administrative assistants and, when enabled, the employee receive copies of completed forms and evidence by email.

Our service providers include Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database), Cloudflare R2 (private files), Resend (transactional email), GitHub Actions (encrypted backup processing), and Google (support email). They process information to provide these services. Data may be processed in the United States and other locations where these providers operate. We may disclose information when required by applicable law.

Storage and retention

Saved profile and inspection data are encrypted on the server. Files use private storage and controlled access; backup files are additionally encrypted. HTTPS protects data in transit. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your profile remains while your account is active. Closed trip forms, photos and receipts are scheduled for deletion 60 days after return or cancellation, including removal from recoverable backups. Active trips remain until closed or an account deletion request is processed. Daily cleanup may take up to 24 hours after expiry. Anonymous vehicle mileage and availability may remain without an identifiable staff profile.

Delete your account and associated data

In the app or website, open My profile → Delete my account. Review the consequences, type DELETE and confirm. You may need to sign in again to verify your identity. Access is revoked immediately; your profile, signed forms, photos, receipts and generated reports are removed through a retryable cleanup process, including recoverable backups, within 7 days. Keep the private receipt to check completion at Deletion status.

This is permanent. A vehicle still checked out is held for inspection. Associated records are deleted earlier than the usual 60-day period when you request account deletion. Copies already emailed, exported or downloaded are outside the app’s control: contact their recipients or your organization to request their deletion. Service-provider security logs and residual infrastructure copies follow the providers’ limited retention schedules and are not used to restore deleted accounts.

Your choices and device permissions

Update your saved profile in My profile. Camera access is requested only when taking evidence photos or using Apple’s document scanner. You choose photos to upload using the system picker. You can revoke camera access in device settings. The app clears its session and temporary private files when you sign out; copies you explicitly save or share remain where you saved them.

Contact and changes

For access, corrections, deletion help or privacy questions, email fleetcheckout.support@gmail.com. Do not email passwords or sign-in codes. Fleet Checkout is for staff and is not directed to children. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
