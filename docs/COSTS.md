# Operating costs and service limits

Verified against the official pages linked below on **November 5, 2026**. Prices are USD. These limits can change; check the links again before opening an account or renewing a plan. No paid service, trial, payment method, or automatic upgrade is enabled by this repository.

## Selected deployment

Run one Node.js application on an **existing, organization-approved always-on computer or Linux VM**, with SQLite on its local persistent disk, private photo files, and an approved organization SMTP relay. The server is shared by every iPad; the database is not stored separately on each iPad. Serve it over HTTPS through the organization's existing reverse proxy or Caddy. Use individual administrator-invited accounts until the organization supplies an approved identity-provider integration.

This has **$0 recurring application-software license cost**, provided the organization already has the server, storage, backup destination, network, domain or approved internal certificate, and permitted SMTP service. Electricity, hardware replacement, IT maintenance, domain registration, external hosting, storage expansion, and an email license can still cost money. A laptop that sleeps or leaves the building is not an appropriate production server. No deployment or organization credentials are assumed to exist.

The decision favors one deployable service and durable inspection evidence. It does not promise that a free managed cloud stack will retain photographs indefinitely.

## Photo and report storage estimate

**Planning assumptions, not measured usage:** eight mandatory exterior views per completed trip (four checkout, four new return), plus two additional damage/interior/odometer/receipt images. Estimate each original at **3 MB**, each normalized/stamped viewing copy at **0.4 MB**, and the saved forms, signatures, metadata and report copies at **2 MB per completed trip**. Decimal MB/GB are used. Actual iPad image sizes and PDF embedding vary; measure the first 20 real trips and revise the estimate.

```
Minimum eight photos: 8 × (3 + 0.4) + 2 = 29.2 MB/trip
Planning ten photos: 10 × (3 + 0.4) + 2 = 36 MB/trip
100 trips/month: 3.6 GB/month = 43.2 GB/year of primary evidence
500 trips/month: 18 GB/month = 216 GB/year of primary evidence
One additional full backup: approximately doubles those figures
```

At the ten-photo estimate, **1 GB holds about 27 completed trips** before unrelated files, deleted-object retention, revisions or overhead. Even the minimum eight photos yield only about 34 trips. The 1 GB free allowance is a lifetime storage ceiling until evidence is exported or deleted under an approved policy, not a monthly replenishment.

A 100 GB primary allocation with 20% operating headroom leaves 80 GB, or about **2,222 trips / 22 months at 100 trips per month**. A separately stored full backup needs approximately another 80 GB at that point. Multiple backup generations, revisions and unusually large photos require more. A stamped copy does not replace the original. Attaching originals to every email is unnecessary and can exceed mailbox message limits; printable reports and the private gallery provide review access.

The application must stop accepting new uploads when its configured storage budget is reached, make the problem visible, and preserve existing evidence. **Never automatically delete signed inspections to stay on a free plan.** The organization decides retention periods and any eventual deletion after export and review.

## Email capacity estimate

Each trip causes a checkout notification and a return notification containing both forms. With one recipient and no employee copy, that is **two recipient-deliveries/trip**: a theoretical Resend maximum of 1,500 trips/month or 50/day before invitations, retries, resends and other traffic. Adding one employee copy makes four deliveries/trip: 750/month or 25/day. Additional configured recipients reduce capacity proportionally. Leave a substantial margin; quota exhaustion must leave reports saved and notifications visible for retry.

Email provider acceptance only means the provider accepted the message. It does not establish inbox delivery or that a person read it. Delivery-confirmation integration depends on the provider. Mailbox retention and attachment copies also consume storage outside the application's disk estimate.

## Items to confirm before production

1. Actual number of vehicles, trips per month, average image size and evidence retention period.
2. Approved always-on host, persistent disk size, HTTPS address/certificate, and private backup destination.
3. Existing organization identity provider and whether an app integration is allowed without a new license. The delivered invited-account workflow does not claim that SSO has been configured.
4. Organization SMTP relay authorization, sending limits, sender identity and actual notification recipients; or approval to configure a separate provider.
5. An administrator responsible for capacity alerts, failed notifications, updates, retention decisions and restore testing.

If those resources are not already available, a paid host, additional storage, domain or email plan may be necessary. There is no guarantee of unlimited free operation.
